24 effect files in the previous port build were not those originals (several were duplicates of
each other, and transport-ship / conquered / the click set did not match). They were replaced with
the bytes from that commit. 10 effect files and all 4 ambience files already matched and were kept.
Hashes are in docs/audio_measurements.json.

NOT included from OpenFront: resources are the only open set. OpenFront's music and
game-start-alert live under proprietary/ and are All Rights Reserved. They are not in this project.

sounds/music/frontline_pad.ogg is not from OpenFront. It is an original synthesized pad written for
this port (public-domain dedication by the port). It is the only file the game plays as music.

sounds/rejected/roworld_menu.mp3 and roworld_war.mp3 are the previous "cleaned" theme encodes.
They are kept so the failure can be checked, and the game does not load them. See
sounds/rejected/README.txt and docs/AUDIO.md.
